What this range does—and does not—establish

All bounds describe reference positions within the supplied eight-point error sets. No coordinates are corrected and no error model is fitted here. A lower bound above tolerance establishes separation under those sets; an upper bound below tolerance establishes agreement under them. An unavailable or unresolved relation is not counted as agreement. A matched-relation fraction is not facial resemblance probability.

Distances, unsigned angles and bilateral contrasts are reflection-insensitive. Oriented areas and the proper-rotation shape distance retain reflection sensitivity under fixed point correspondence. The finite directional calculations use conservative corrections and float64 padding; they are not exact extrema or formally verified interval arithmetic.

Statistical coverage transfers only under the original source-calibration assumptions. A new acquisition domain or a poorly generalized detector is not repaired by these geometric bounds.
