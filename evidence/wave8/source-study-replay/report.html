Evidence constraints
  • Some frozen acquisition categories lack a finite deployable radius. Queries in them are rejected without pooled fallback; evaluation retains them.
  • Category membership is declared acquisition metadata. Matching a label does not detect hidden within-category distribution shift.
  • At least one evaluation stratum has no calibration groups: no deployment coverage transfer is justified for that stratum.
  • Empirical aggregate coverage is below the nominal target; this is reported, not repaired using evaluation labels.
  • Every observation in this study is a controlled synthetic fixture, not live human-landmark inference.
